# Skin Profile — direction contract
Mode: Operate through the 10-question flow, Read for the result.
User-pinned structure: mobile-first, one single-select question per screen, Back/Next, complete results before optional email, separate marketing consent and optional research. Route-local minimal header/footer; self-hosted Google Sans. Mineral black/ivory palette (#080808 / #e5e0d6, secondary #b7b2a8, controls #dbd6ca, rules #30302e), no background films or decorative gradients.
First viewport: “Το δέρμα σου μιλάει.” followed by “Έχεις μάθει να το ακούς;”, a fluid 34–43px mobile headline, an asymmetric face portrait alongside desktop copy and beneath mobile copy, a 10-question/free/no-registration caption and one primary start action. No score, product recommendation or email barrier.
Signature interaction: a thin progress rule follows the current question as the visitor explicitly advances or goes back; native radio rows invert to ivory with dark text when selected. Back preserves choices; completing the flow after changing an answer re-evaluates the deterministic result. Buttons use 160ms feedback, answer rows 180ms background/color, progress width 320ms. Question content enters over 320ms without delaying navigation. Photography enters over 700–850ms from an already visible state. Reduced motion disables all entrance animations and transitions.
Quiz body: 740px maximum outer width including 48px desktop/22px mobile gutters, >=58px mobile answer targets, visible focus, 17px base text and 16px mobile answer text. Results: morning/evening protocols visible immediately, with native disclosures for reasons, ingredient explanations, habits and specialist guidance. No diagnosis. Discomfort overrides all promotional priorities.
Privacy: raw answers exist only in tab memory; no profile in URLs, browser storage, market research or analytics. Email is an explicit one-time action. Marketing never follows result delivery implicitly. Research is anonymous and separate, after product-interest action, with skip controls.
Value First sequence: full educational results → optional guide disclosure (“Η φροντίδα σου δεν τελειώνει εδώ.” / “Στείλε μου τον οδηγό”) → development concept (“Η περιποίηση έγινε περίπλοκη. Εμείς επιλέγουμε μια άλλη κατεύθυνση.”) → optional research invitation (“Η δική σου άποψη έχει θέση εδώ”) → optional research. The independent marketing disclosure (“Κάτι απλό παίρνει μορφή.”) offers educational content and development/launch updates with its own unchecked consent. Product imagery is explicitly illustrative of a concept under development, with no purchase obligation. Guide delivery is never a prerequisite for viewing the concept or joining updates.
Measurement boundary: the internal funnel reports consented aggregate events, not unique or identified leads. Guide requests do not establish successful delivery or marketing subscription; interest clicks do not establish purchases. `qualified_lead` and `customer` remain unmeasured/NULL. Research has no identified joins; usual spending is not willingness to pay. Randomized price research is deferred. The dedicated marketing purpose is `skin-profile-education-launch-v1`, with separate consent receipts preserving legacy signup consent; its receiver revision and privacy review remain release dependencies.
Build path: code-led; user specified structure, color, controls and editorial reference. No approved visual comp. Existing source photography only. No new visual-world exploration or generated imagery.
Preservation boundary: the mineral palette, divider-based answer rows, 6px controls and 12px photographs, minimal wordmark/return header and copyright/privacy/contact footer are local to `.profile-page` and its `profile-*` components in `public/skin-profile/profile.css`. The route reuses incumbent self-hosted Google Sans and owner photography. Shared CSS, global design tokens, `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` are unchanged by this surface.
Review scope: draft implementation; do not publish production before owner content review, as explicitly requested in uploaded brief.

Homepage discovery extension: one scoped section immediately before FAQ/signup is labelled “01 / DISCOVER”, “THE PRIMAL SKIN PROFILE”, with a rounded “Κάνε το τεστ” link. Its 90-second description and the rest of the homepage remain unchanged by the route redesign. The route start control shows an explicit inline 18+ error instead of relying only on browser validation.
