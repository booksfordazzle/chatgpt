# Skin Profile — direction contract
Mode: Operate through the 10-question flow, Read for the result.
User-pinned structure: mobile-first, one single-select question per screen, Back/Next, complete results before optional email, separate marketing consent and optional research. Existing fonts/header/footer; new route only. Flat obsidian/ivory/sand palette, no background films or decorative gradients.
First viewport: the supplied Greek question as a 36px mobile headline, one real face photograph on desktop, a quiet 90-second promise and one primary start action. No score, product recommendation or email barrier.
Signature interaction: a thin progress rule follows the current question as the visitor explicitly advances or goes back; native radio rows reveal a selected dot and a sand border. Back preserves choices; completing the flow after changing an answer re-evaluates the deterministic result. Buttons and answer rows use 180ms background/color/border transitions; WebKit progress width also transitions over 180ms. Reduced motion disables these transitions.
Quiz body: 600px maximum, 22px mobile gutters, >=56px answer targets, visible focus, 17px body. Results: readable morning/evening protocols, ingredient explanations, reasons drawn from answers, no diagnosis. Discomfort overrides all promotional priorities.
Privacy: raw answers exist only in tab memory; no profile in URLs, browser storage, market research or analytics. Email is an explicit one-time action. Marketing never follows result delivery implicitly. Research is anonymous and separate, after product-interest action, with skip controls.
Value First sequence: full educational results → optional guide delivery (“Κράτησε τη ρουτίνα σου.” / “Στείλε μου τον οδηγό”) → development concept (“Η περιποίηση, πίσω στα ουσιώδη.”) → optional expressed interest (“Ενδιαφέρομαι να το γνωρίσω”) → optional research. The independent marketing form offers educational content and development/launch updates with its own unchecked consent. Product imagery is explicitly illustrative of a concept under development, with no purchase obligation. Guide delivery is never a prerequisite for viewing the concept or joining updates.
Measurement boundary: the internal funnel reports consented aggregate events, not unique or identified leads. Guide requests do not establish successful delivery or marketing subscription; interest clicks do not establish purchases. `qualified_lead` and `customer` remain unmeasured/NULL. Research has no identified joins; usual spending is not willingness to pay. Randomized price research is deferred. The dedicated marketing purpose is `skin-profile-education-launch-v1`, with separate consent receipts preserving legacy signup consent; its receiver revision and privacy review remain release dependencies.
Build path: code-led; user specified structure, color, controls and editorial reference. No approved visual comp. Existing source photography only. No new visual-world exploration or generated imagery.
Preservation boundary: the flat palette and square-cornered quiz controls/photographs are local to `.profile-page` and its `profile-*` components in `public/skin-profile/profile.css`. The route inherits self-hosted Google Sans, the existing header/navigation and footer from the incumbent site. Shared CSS, global design tokens, `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` are unchanged by this surface.
Review scope: draft implementation; do not publish production before owner content review, as explicitly requested in uploaded brief.
