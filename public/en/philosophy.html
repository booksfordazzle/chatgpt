Ancestral
skincare.

As early as the fifth century BC, Hippocrates used purified animal fat (tallow) as a base for healing ointments.

Centuries later, Dioscorides, in his work De Materia Medica, recorded the effects of Chios mastic on skin regeneration.

When did everything
get so complicated?

Most creams today are full of synthetic ingredients and artificial fragrances you can barely pronounce.

You do not need 30 ingredients.

You only need the ones that work.

Subtraction is evolution.

We do not seek to recreate the past.
We draw on its inspiration to create a modern ancestral balm.
For us, evolution does not always mean adding more.

Sometimes it means returning to what truly matters.
