How do I use PRIMAL CORE?

Warm a small amount between your palms and apply to a clean face, morning or evening. If your skin is very dry, start with a tiny amount at night and see how it feels in the morning. For external use only — avoid the eye area and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Is it for dry skin?

Yes, it was made for dry or stressed skin, helping reduce the appearance of dryness and fine lines. For combination skin, use it only on dry areas or in smaller amounts to avoid weighing down oily zones.

How long does a 50 ml jar last?

Several weeks when using a small amount daily. The exact duration depends on how much you apply and whether you use it once or twice a day — a little goes a long way, as the balm spreads easily when warmed between your palms.

Does it have a scent?

Chios mastic provides the signature scent — clean, fresh, resinous and slightly woody.
