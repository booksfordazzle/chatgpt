Less dryness. A smoother appearance.

After washing, shaving or exposure to cold weather, your skin can feel dry and tight, while dehydration can make fine lines and wrinkles more noticeable.

A small amount of PRIMAL CORE helps retain moisture, leaving your skin soft, supple and smoother-looking, while also helping reduce the visibility of fine lines.

Beef tallow

Beef tallow intensively nourishes and repairs the skin barrier, providing deep hydration through fatty acids and vitamins that resemble the skin's natural lipids.

Chios mastic oil

Chios mastic oil offers strong antioxidant and antibacterial protection, soothes redness and helps regenerate and firm the skin, giving it a healthy appearance and natural radiance.

Beeswax

Beeswax creates a protective film on the skin that locks in moisture and soothes irritation, leaving the face soft and hydrated.

Jojoba oil

Jojoba oil deeply moisturizes and regulates oiliness by mimicking natural sebum without clogging pores.

Vitamin E

Vitamin E (Tocopherol) is known for its antioxidant properties. It helps protect the skin from free radicals, helping maintain a healthy, well-cared-for appearance.

How do I use PRIMAL CORE?

Warm a small amount between your palms and apply to a clean face, morning or evening. If your skin is very dry, start with a tiny amount at night and see how it feels in the morning. For external use only — avoid the eye area and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Is it for dry skin?

Yes, it was made for dry or stressed skin, helping reduce the appearance of dryness and fine lines. For combination skin, use it only on dry areas or in smaller amounts to avoid weighing down oily zones.

How long does a 50 ml jar last?

Several weeks when using a small amount daily. The exact duration depends on how much you apply and whether you use it once or twice a day — a little goes a long way, as the balm spreads easily when warmed between your palms.

Does it have a scent?

Chios mastic provides the signature scent — clean, fresh, resinous and slightly woody.
