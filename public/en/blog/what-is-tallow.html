What Is Beef Tallow?

A traditional raw material in modern skincare.

Beef tallow is cattle fat that has been separated from fatty tissue and purified through appropriate processing. Depending on composition and temperature, it has a solid or semisolid texture.

Its role in a cosmetic

It consists mainly of triglycerides containing fatty acids such as oleic, palmitic and stearic acids. Their proportions vary with the raw material and processing.

In a cosmetic, it can serve as a lipid base that contributes structure and an emollient feel. The result depends on the entire formula, not just one ingredient.

What natural origin does not prove

Natural origin does not guarantee safety, effectiveness or suitability for every skin type. Tallow does not have the same composition as human sebum and should not be regarded as a treatment for skin conditions.

Raw material quality, stability and safety assessment of the final formula matter. Raw cooking fat is not a substitute for an assessed cosmetic.
