Data controller

What this page collects

If you choose to join the waitlist, we collect your email address, registration time and a record of your consent. The form only asks for your email. It does not offer accounts, orders or payments.

Why we collect it

We use this information to send you updates about the PRIMAL CORE launch. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing hello@primalcore.gr.

How it is stored

The form sends your information through our hosting server to Google Apps Script for storage in a private Google Sheet. The hosting provider and Google process data to provide these services. We record your email, consent receipt time, consent wording and privacy-policy versions, language, coarse device type (mobile or desktop), signup source and registration time. Where present, we also record campaign tags, the landing-page path and the referring site origin, without its query parameters. Hosting logs may include your IP address and the time of the request. Signup confirmation appears on a separate website page. No automatic welcome email is sent.

Retention and your rights

The list information is used for the purpose above while your consent remains active. You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing and, where applicable, portability of your data. For requests and questions, write to hello@primalcore.gr. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Cookies and external resources

This website's code does not install analytics or advertising cookies and does not use tracking tools. Fonts and media are hosted with the website.
