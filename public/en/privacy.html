Privacy policy.

PRIMAL CORE launch updates.

Data controller

MMTX E.E, 12 Filikis Etaireias Street. For questions or requests about your personal data, contact ermitikos@gmail.com.

What this page collects

If you choose to join the waitlist, we collect your email address, registration time and a record of your consent. The form only asks for your email. It does not offer accounts, orders or payments.

Why we collect it

We use this information to send you updates about the PRIMAL CORE launch. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing ermitikos@gmail.com.

How it is stored

The signup form sends data through our hosting server to Google Apps Script for storage in a private Google Sheet. Existing email, registration time, consent records, policy identifiers, language and source fields remain unchanged. Campaign parameters and first-entry URLs are held in sessionStorage for this browser tab and sent with your signup, together with device type, operating system, browser category and viewport width. No attribution cookies, cross-session visitor ID, precise location or raw user-agent string are collected. Security rate-limit counters remain separate from signup attribution. No automatic welcome email is sent.

Retention and your rights

The list information is used for the purpose above while your consent remains active. You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing and, where applicable, portability of your data. For requests and questions, write to ermitikos@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

To limit repeated signup attempts, the server temporarily holds salted hashes of email addresses and platform-provided IP addresses in memory for up to 10 minutes. These counters are not written to the subscriber spreadsheet.

Technical information and attribution

We collect technical information about your device type, operating system, browser, screen size, landing page, and how you arrived at our site (UTM parameters and referrer) for analytics and to improve the signup experience.

Attribution is first-party and session-only. We keep the first landing pathname and query string and the full entry referrer URL when the browser makes it available. Full URLs can contain personal data in their query strings. Session storage is cleared when the browser ends the tab session; information submitted with an email is retained with the signup record under this policy. We do not collect an attribution IP address, geolocation, fingerprint or persistent visitor identifier. Browser settings may limit referrer details and storage.

Contact messages

When you use our contact form, your name, email address and message are processed by FormSubmit and forwarded to ermitikos@gmail.com, hosted by Google, so we can handle your enquiry and reply. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or steps at your request before entering a contract where applicable. The contact form uses a hidden spam honeypot and FormSubmit’s CAPTCHA verification on its own page before returning to our site. FormSubmit and its verification providers may process technical data and use cookies on that external page to prevent abuse. See the FormSubmit privacy policy. Contact messages do not subscribe you to marketing. You can request access or deletion using the contact address above.

Cookies and external resources

This website's code does not install analytics or advertising cookies and does not use tracking tools. Fonts and media are hosted with the website.
