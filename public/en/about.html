Skincare starts with the simple things.

The story of PRIMAL CORE.

How it began

It all began with a question: how did people make cosmetics in the past?

As we researched, we kept coming across tallow — purified beef fat.

This raw material became the starting point for PRIMAL CORE: a men's face balm combining traditional ingredients with a modern approach to formulation.

What we are developing

A water-free face balm based on beef tallow.

Our aim is emollient care, a pleasant texture and simple everyday application.

The product is under development. Its formula, suitability and claims will be finalized after the necessary checks. It is not yet available for sale.

What we believe

  • Every ingredient should serve a specific functional purpose.
  • Daily skincare can be simple.
  • Product promises should be supported by evidence.
