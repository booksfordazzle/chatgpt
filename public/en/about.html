Skincare was never much on my mind.

Until I began noticing the first changes in my face. Fine lines, signs of time.

Like most people, I bought creams at the supermarket without paying much attention to their ingredients. Sometimes my face felt even more dehydrated after applying them.

So I started looking into it.

I wanted to understand what skin really needs, what ingredients do, and how people cared for their skin before the modern cosmetics industry.

Through that search, I discovered beef tallow, a traditional skincare ingredient used for generations.

Its simplicity sparked my curiosity.

Nearly a year of personal research, testing and searching for ingredients followed. My own experience made me believe this was a direction worth exploring further.

Somewhere along the way, PRIMAL CORE was born.

A balm based on beef tallow and Chios mastic, inspired by ancestral Greek skincare and designed around one simple principle:

Know what you put on your skin. And why.

The product is under development. Its formula, suitability and claims will be finalized after the necessary checks. It is not yet available for sale.
