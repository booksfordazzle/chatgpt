# PRIMAL CORE interface

Preserve the supplied dark, cinematic Greek identity: near-black surfaces, ivory typography, product and ingredient photography, restrained moss/olive tones. The home surface helps visitors understand the product's development status and request launch updates. Articles support reading.

Use self-hosted Roboto with Greek and Latin subsets, 17px body text, spacious sections, and a clear type hierarchy. Avoid before/after imagery and guaranteed results. Product development goals must remain distinct from proven finished-product claims.

Navigation is native links to static URLs. FAQs use native details/summary. The waitlist requests one email with unchecked explicit consent. Only confirmed Google storage may produce success. Keep errors actionable and keep entered data on failure. No perpetual animation loops; mobile film playback is optional. Reduced motion always displays a static hero poster.

Tokens and responsive rules are in public/styles.css. Publishing headers are in netlify.toml.
