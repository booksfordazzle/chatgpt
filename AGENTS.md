# Project instructions

Before changing the interface, read DESIGN.md and preserve its saved owner preferences. In particular, keep generous spacing between headings and following lists/media, and between photographs and captions; use the shared CSS spacing tokens. Keep the benefits heading top-aligned and stationary when answers expand. The ingredient video must autoplay muted and loop on encounter, with user pause preserved; keep reduced-motion and accessible fallback behavior.

The homepage product image is intentionally full-bleed and square-cornered; its caption stays within the content grid. The hero uses the original ia.mp4 with desktop scroll seeking; mobile and reduced-motion visitors see its static poster. Ingredient playback remains independent. Preserve the manually operated ingredient slider and its no-JavaScript reading fallback.
